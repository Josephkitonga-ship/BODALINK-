Policies and terms

The rules for using BodaLink, in plain words.

These terms cover riding, sending parcels, riding for BodaLink and how we handle your information. Last updated 17 November 2026.

Using BodaLink

BodaLink is a platform that connects passengers and senders with boda riders. The ride or delivery itself is between you and the rider. By creating an account or booking a trip you agree to these terms.

  • Riders must be 18 or older and have a valid national ID.
  • Give true information when you sign up. Do not use someone else's name, ID or phone number.
  • You are responsible for what happens under your account. Keep your password private.

Booking and paying

  • Riders set their own prices. You see every bid before you accept one, and the fare you accept is the fare you pay.
  • BodaLink does not collect fares. You pay your rider directly, in cash or by M-Pesa, as you agree on the trip.
  • For parcels, the sender or the recipient pays, as chosen when booking.
  • Riders pay BodaLink a day pass to go online. The pass is KSh 100 for 24 hours and is not part of your fare.

Cancelling

  • Passengers can cancel while riders are still bidding, and after a rider has been chosen but before pickup. Once the trip has started it cannot be cancelled in the app.
  • Riders who cannot do a job they accepted should release it before pickup, so the passenger gets bids again.
  • We may review accounts that cancel or release jobs again and again.

Rules for riders

  • Go through verification before taking a first job, and keep your documents and number plate details up to date.
  • Follow Kenyan traffic law, including wearing a helmet, and ride a bike you are allowed to ride.
  • Price jobs honestly and charge the fare the passenger accepted.
  • Keep your day pass active while you are online. Do not accept jobs you cannot complete.
  • Treat passengers and their parcels with care and respect.

Parcels

  • Do not send anything illegal, stolen or dangerous. Riders may refuse a parcel they believe breaks this rule.
  • Describe the parcel and its size honestly, and give the recipient's real name and phone number.
  • Every parcel has a pickup code. The sender gives it to the rider at pickup, so the right rider collects the parcel.

Safety

  • In an emergency, call 999 or 112 first.
  • Check that the rider's name and number plate match what you see in the app before you get on.
  • Share your trip with someone you trust. You can send the trip ticket on WhatsApp from the trip screen.
  • Report any rider or passenger who made you feel unsafe on the contact page. We take reports seriously and may suspend accounts while we look into them.

Privacy

What we collect. Your name, email, phone number and a profile photo. For riders, a national ID number, a photo of the ID and a photo of the bike with its number plate. For passengers, an ID number and emergency contact if you choose to add them. For trips, the pickup and drop-off places, notes, parcel details, the recipient's name and phone number, the fare and ratings. We use your location only when you tap "Use my current location", or, for riders, while you are online and sharing it.

Why we use it. To run trips, check riders, keep people safe, handle problems and support you. We do not sell your information and we do not show adverts based on it.

Who sees what. A passenger and their rider see each other's name and phone number during an active trip. A parcel's recipient details go to the rider only after you accept their bid. Your profile photo is shown to the other person on your trip. A passenger sees the photos of riders who bid, and a rider sees a passenger's photo once the passenger chooses them. BodaLink staff can see all photos. ID and bike photos are private, and only BodaLink staff who review riders can open them. None of it appears on public pages.

Cookies and storage. We use your browser's storage to keep you signed in. We do not use advertising cookies.

Your rights. Kenya's Data Protection Act, 2019 gives you rights over your personal data, including to see it, correct it and ask for it to be deleted. To use them, contact us. We may need to keep some records, for example to settle a dispute or to meet a legal duty.

Accounts

We may suspend or close an account that breaks these terms, uses false details, or puts other people at risk. You can ask us to close your account at any time through the contact page.

Our responsibility

BodaLink connects you with riders and does not provide the transport itself. Riders are independent and are not BodaLink employees. We work to keep the service running and to check riders, but we cannot promise it will always be available or that every trip will go to plan.

Changes and questions

We may update these terms as BodaLink grows. The date at the top shows the latest version. If you have a question about them, use the contact page.
