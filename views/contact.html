Ask a question, report a problem with a trip, or find out how to ride with us. We reply as soon as we can.

Before you get in touch

  • In an emergency, call 999 or 112 first.
  • For a problem with a trip, have the trip ticket code ready. It looks like BL-AB12 and is on your trip screen.
  • New riders are reviewed within a day. If you are waiting, open your checklist in the app to see what is missing.
